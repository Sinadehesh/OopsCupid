# Short-form video scripts

Fifteen scripts, one per quiz. Written for TikTok, Reels and Shorts —
vertical, 30–50 seconds, no editing skills required.

**Why this channel and not SEO or ads.** Paid traffic does not work at a
€9.99 price point: you can afford roughly €0.20 a visitor and relationship
CPCs run €0.30–1.50. SEO for the category terms is owned by Healthline and
Psychology Today. Short-form is the only channel where a new account with
no audience and no budget can reach 50,000 people in a week, and quiz
content is unusually well suited to it — the format has a built-in comment
prompt.

## How to use these

- **Hook is the whole game.** The first 1.5 seconds decide everything. Say
  the hook line before you are fully in frame if you have to. Do not open
  with "hi guys".
- **Film it once, badly.** A phone, a window, no editing. Over-produced
  performs worse in this niche — it reads as an ad.
- **One idea per video.** The script gives you one. Resist adding.
- **Put the CTA in the caption, not the video.** Saying "link in bio"
  out loud suppresses reach on every platform. The video ends on the
  question; the caption carries the link.
- **Post the same script three times** across two weeks with different
  hooks. The algorithm is a lottery and the ticket is cheap.
- **Reply to comments with a second video.** This is the single highest
  leverage habit — a reply video inherits the parent's reach.

## Tracking

Use `?ref=tt` on the link in bio (e.g. `oopscupid.com/is-he-gaslighting-me?ref=tt`).
Vercel Analytics breaks results down by `utmSource` and referrer, so you
will be able to see which script actually sent people.

## 1 — "Is he gaslighting me?"

**Hook (0–2s):** "If you leave every argument apologising, this is for you."

**Beats:**
- "There's a name for it. It's called DARVO — deny, attack, reverse victim and offender."
- "It runs in three moves and takes about a minute."
- "One: it didn't happen. Two: something about your tone, your timing, your history. Three: now he's the one who's been wronged."
- "And you apologise. Not for the original thing — for the last ten minutes. Which was genuinely awful. That's why it works."

**End on:** "Does that sequence sound familiar?"

**On-screen text:** `DENY → ATTACK → REVERSE`

**Caption:** The reason you can't explain how the conversation got there. Free 8-minute assessment in bio.

## 2 — "Is he manipulative?"

**Hook:** "Three words that end a conversation without answering it: 'you're too sensitive.'"

**Beats:**
- "It's not always manipulation. Sometimes it's true."
- "Here's the test. After he says it — does he still answer what you actually raised?"
- "If yes, that's a person disagreeing with you. That's allowed."
- "If the conversation is now entirely about your sensitivity and the original thing never comes back — that phrase isn't describing you. It's protecting him."

**End on:** "Which one is it in your house?"

**On-screen text:** `Does the original issue survive?`

**Caption:** One test, no drama. Assessment in bio.

## 3 — "Is he cheating?"

**Hook:** "Stop checking his phone. I'll tell you what actually matters."

**On-screen text:** `CHANGE > BEHAVIOUR`

**Caption:** The timeline tells you more than the phone would. Free assessment in bio.

## 7 — "What's my partner's attachment style?"

**Hook:** "If he pulls away right after a good week — that's not random."

**Beats:**
- "Avoidant withdrawal is triggered by closeness, not by conflict."

**End on:** "Does yours withdraw after closeness or after conflict?"

**On-screen text:** `Closeness is the trigger`

## The reply-video habit

This matters more than any single script above. When a video gets
comments, the highest-performing follow-up is a reply video — it inherits
the parent's reach and costs you thirty seconds.

Three that always work in this niche:

1. **"What if I'm the toxic one?"** — asked on every video. Answer: the
   fact that you're asking is informative; people who are rarely do. Then
   give one real check.
2. **"What do I actually say?"** — pull one script straight from a premium
   report. Give it away. It demonstrates the product better than describing
   it does.
3. **Anyone disagreeing.** Agree with the part that's right, then hold the
   part that isn't. Disagreement in comments is reach.

## Posting cadence

One video a day for thirty days beats five a day for a week. The account
needs a consistent signal more than it needs volume, and you need to
survive the first three weeks — which will look like nothing is happening,
because nothing is.

Expect: weeks 1–2 flat, week 3 one video does 10× the others for no
discernible reason, weeks 4+ it starts compounding. If nothing has moved
by day 30, change the hooks, not the topic.
