# TikTok with Gemini: setup, master prompt, example clips

## What you set up (once)

1. **TikTok account** named `oopscupid`. In Settings > Account, switch to a
   **Business account** (Personal accounts need 1,000 followers before a link
   in the bio is allowed).
2. **Bio:** `Is it him, or is it you? Free relationship tests 👇`
3. **Website link:** `https://oopscupid.com/things-he-says?utm_source=tiktok`
   (the `utm_source` lets the site count TikTok visitors).
4. **Tools:** Gemini (Veo) for clips, CapCut (free) to join the clips and add
   the on-screen text. Veo writes text on screen badly, so text goes in last,
   in CapCut.
5. **Each post:** switch on "AI-generated content" when posting. TikTok requires
   it for realistic AI video and hides unlabelled ones.

## The rules that keep the account alive

- Sultry and romantic is fine: tension, glances, a hand on a waist, rain,
  late-night texts. Nothing sexual: no nudity, lingerie, sex acts or
  suggestive close-ups of bodies. TikTok hides that from the For You page.
- Characters look clearly 25 or older.
- Stories are "POV" or "storytime" fiction. Never present an AI character as a
  real customer or a real review, and use no invented numbers.
- Never say "therapist", "clinical" or "diagnose".

## Master prompt (paste into Gemini as it is)

```
You are a viral TikTok storyteller and director for OopsCupid (oopscupid.com), a
site with free relationship tests for women in their 20s and 30s: "is he
manipulating me", "is he cheating", "why do I pick bad guys", "my attachment
style", "is my friend toxic". The goal of every video is one action: the
viewer taps the link in bio and takes a free test.

Create 5 TikTok video concepts. For EACH concept give me:

1. TITLE and the TEST it leads to (pick from the list above).
2. HOOK: the on-screen text for the first 1.5 seconds. It must be a
   specific, slightly uncomfortable truth she recognises from her own
   relationship, written as if spoken to her ("POV: he said 'that never
   happened' and you believed him"). No questions like "Have you ever...".
3. STORY: 3 beats of about 8 seconds each. Beat 1 is the romance or the
   tension (make her feel the pull of him). Beat 2 is the red flag moment,
   small and real, the kind she has lived. Beat 3 is the realisation, her
   face as it lands. End on an open loop, not a resolution.
4. VEO PROMPTS: one prompt per beat for Google Veo, 8 seconds, vertical 9:16.
   Every prompt must start with the SAME character description, word for
   word, so the characters look identical across clips:
   "Maya, a woman in her late 20s with dark wavy shoulder-length hair, gold
   hoop earrings, cream knit sweater. Daniel, a man in his early 30s, short
   dark hair, light stubble, black t-shirt."
   Then describe: setting, camera move (slow push-in, handheld close-up,
   over-the-shoulder), lighting (warm lamp, blue phone glow at 2am, rain on
   a window, golden hour), one small action, and the sound (ambient room
   tone, rain, a phone buzz, a single short line of dialogue if needed).
   Cinematic, moody, intimate, like a Netflix romance drama. Sultry and
   romantic tension is allowed; no nudity, no lingerie, nothing sexual,
   both clearly adults over 25. No text or captions inside the video.
5. ON-SCREEN TEXT for each beat (short, lowercase, one line, I add it in
   CapCut).
6. END CARD text (last 2 seconds): a line that turns her curiosity into the
   click, for example "he's said 3 of these. how many has yours? free test,
   link in bio". Never promise a diagnosis.
7. CAPTION with one question that makes her comment ("which one has he said
   to you?") plus 4 hashtags from: #relationshiptok #redflags #toxicrelationship
   #attachmentstyle #gaslighting #datingadvice #situationship #fyp.
8. PART 2 idea, so the video can become a series.

Make the 5 concepts different emotions: love-bombing that goes cold, the
2am phone-checking spiral, "that never happened" gaslighting, the nice guy
who felt "boring", and the best friend who is only happy for you when
you're doing worse than her. Write in British English. Keep all dialogue
under 10 words per line.
```

## Posting rhythm

One video a day for 14 days, around 7 to 9pm. Post the same story twice with
different hooks. After 14 days, keep making more of the 2 to 3 videos with the
most link taps (TikTok Analytics > Content), and drop the rest. Reply to
comments with video replies: they reach new people for free.
